The preferred internal baseline is
`Hyponoia_v3_memory_bloom_smooth_D5_20260820_114211.wav`. Its Critic scores are
not treated as aesthetic truth; the listener's judgement is primary.

The associated render used 145 layers, 62 unique sound objects and an estimated

## Rejected direction

The v2.4 test render used 131 layers but only 14 unique objects, producing an
89% reuse ratio. Together with explicit midrange reinforcement, this made the
mix perceptually closed and muddy. That direction was rolled back and is not
part of this revision.

## Bridge design

Revision `2026-08-21-d5-aesthetic-bridge-5.1` retains the corrected Critic v2.1,
level-specific comments, continuity edge protection and a small amount of
energy/synthetic selection from the current system. It restores the earlier
aesthetic through:

- the broader 16-recording / 30-object soft material plan;
- more exploratory weighted selection;
- elastic, only lightly pulse-attracted timing;
- reduced internal amplitude modulation;
- near-original phrase stretch, envelopes and ambient level;
- a stable central bloom and a long 36-second resolution fade;
- moderate rather than compulsory material recurrence.

With the current D5 activity setting, the structural calculation produces
approximately 141 planned events, close to the 145-layer preferred baseline.
The decisive validation was local listening with the private 103-recording
library. The accepted-direction D5 was judged independently before its Critic
report was considered.

The 5.1 feedback extension adds explicit level-routed interpretations for
`more musical`, `more rhythmic` and `greater bloom`. Together with the existing
`more synthetic` action, the complete listener comment now produces four
bounded D5-only control updates. Rhythmicity changes gentle pulse attraction
and phrase activity without imposing hard quantisation.
